# Closing Kit — From "I Like It" to Money in Hand

Copy-paste messages for every step after a prospect says yes. Don't improvise the money parts — send these.

## 1. The deposit ask (send the moment they approve the mockup)

> Awesome — glad you like it! Here's how it works: $350 flat total. $175 now to start, $175 when the full site is done and you've approved it. If you don't love the finished site, you don't pay the second half. I take Zelle / Venmo / CashApp — whichever is easiest for you. Once the deposit lands I'll need: your best photos (10-20 phone pics are perfect), your services/menu with prices, and your hours. Site's done within a week — usually faster.

*(Adjust the payment apps to whichever you actually have set up. Set them up today if you haven't — all free.)*

## 2. The scope message (send right after the deposit arrives — this is your contract)

> Deposit received — thank you! To confirm what's included: up to 4 pages (home, services/menu, about, contact), mobile-friendly, free hosting, done within 7 days of getting your photos and info. Your only other cost ever is your domain (~$12/year, paid directly to the registrar — I'll help you set it up). Anything beyond that (online ordering, booking systems, extra pages) I'm happy to quote separately. Sound right?

Once they reply "yes" — that reply IS your written agreement. Screenshot it.

## 3. Chasing materials (if 3+ days pass without their photos/info)

> Hi [name]! Still excited to finish your site — just waiting on those photos and your services list. Even rough phone pics work; I'll make them look good. Want me to just build it from what's on your Google/Facebook and you correct anything after?

*(That last offer usually unsticks it — say yes to it yourself: bring me their public info and I'll build from that.)*

## 4. The approval + final payment ask

> Your site's done: [link]. Look it over on your phone and computer. Anything you want changed, tell me — tweaks are included. When you're happy, the final $175 wraps it up, and then I'll connect your domain so it's live at your own address (yourbusiness.com).

**Rule: the site moves to their own domain only after final payment.** Until then it lives on your link, where you control it.

## 5. The handoff + upsell (after final payment)

> All yours — congrats! 🎉 Your site is live at [domain]. A couple things most of my clients add later, whenever you're ready: a Google Business tune-up so you show up better on Maps ($100), and a review card with a QR code so happy customers leave you Google reviews ($150). And if you ever want edits without touching anything yourself, I do a $50/month plan — any changes within 48 hours. No pressure on any of it. Enjoy the site, and if you know another business owner who needs one, send them my way — I'll take $50 off your care plan or next project for every referral.

## 6. The referral follow-up (2 weeks after handoff)

> Hi [name]! Quick check-in — site running well? One favor: if you're happy with how it turned out, would you mind leaving me a quick Google review / letting me use [Business Name] as a reference? It's how I get my next customers, same as you. Thanks!

## Money rules (repeat of pricing.md, because they matter most at closing time)

1. No work beyond the free mockup until the deposit is in your account. Zero exceptions.
2. The scope message defines the job. Extras are quoted, never absorbed.
3. Final payment before the domain connects. Every time.
4. Save every payment confirmation and approval message.
