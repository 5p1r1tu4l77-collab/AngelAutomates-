# Day 1 Checklist — Do These Today (~60-90 min total)

Everything below is free to join. Never pay to sign up for work — anyone asking for money upfront is a scam.

## Part 1: AI-training platform applications (~45 min)

These pay the best for someone with coding skills, but approval takes days to weeks — which is exactly why they go in TODAY. Apply to all four; take whichever approves first.

**IMPORTANT:** Do the assessments yourself, without AI help. They detect it, ban for it, and it kills the whole opportunity. You have real coding skills — that's enough.

- [ ] **DataAnnotation** — https://www.dataannotation.tech/coding
  - Best fit: coding track pays $25-45+/hr, weekly PayPal payouts, most stable of the group.
  - Sign up, then complete the starter assessment when it appears (untimed, take it carefully — quality is everything here).
- [ ] **Outlier AI** — https://outlier.ai
  - $22-35/hr for coders. Higher variance than DataAnnotation but can pay more when projects are flowing.
- [ ] **Alignerr** — https://alignerr.com
  - Similar AI-training work; approval odds improve by having applications everywhere.
- [ ] **Mercor** — https://mercor.com
  - Interview-based; upload a resume and do their AI interview.

Assessment tips (prep, not cheating):
- Write clearly and explain your reasoning — these platforms grade *communication about code* as much as code.
- Take untimed assessments slowly. A careful 90 minutes beats a rushed 20.
- Check grammar/spelling; many rejections are for sloppy writing, not weak code.

## Part 2: Fast-payout fillers (~20 min)

Smaller money, but it lands within days while the above are pending.

- [ ] **Prolific** — https://www.prolific.com
  - Research studies, minimum $8/hr, often more. Spots open in waves — check the site 2-3x/day for the first few days.
  - After your first 4 cashouts you unlock instant cashouts.
- [ ] **UserTesting** — https://www.usertesting.com/get-paid-to-test
  - $10 per 20-min recorded website test (~$30/hr while testing). Do the short practice test carefully — it gates everything.
  - Needs a quiet room + microphone. Payout ~7 days after each test.
