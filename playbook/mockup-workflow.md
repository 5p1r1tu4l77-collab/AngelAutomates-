# Free-Mockup Workflow — Reply to Live Link in Under 30 Minutes

The free mockup is what converts. Speed is the whole trick: a prospect who gets a live link the same hour they replied feels like they hired a pro; one who waits two days goes cold.

## When a prospect replies "sure, show me"

**Step 1 — Grab their info (5 min).** From their Google Maps listing collect:
- Business name, category, city
- Phone number, address, hours
- Star rating + review count
- 2-3 short quotes from their actual reviews (real reviews on the mockup = instant "that's MY business" reaction)
- What they sell / are known for (skim reviews for what customers rave about)

**Step 2 — Tell Claude (1 min).** Paste it all into a Claude Code session with:
> "Build a mockup from templates/mockup/ for [info dump]. Put it in mockups/<business-slug>/ and push."

Claude fills every `{{PLACEHOLDER}}`, picks the right color preset (presets are listed at the top of the template file), and pushes. The push auto-deploys.

**Step 3 — Send the link (1 min).** It'll be live at:
`https://5p1r1tu4l77-collab.github.io/AngelAutomates-/mockups/<business-slug>/`

Message to send with it:
> Here you go — a live homepage for [Business Name]: [link]
> Open it on your phone. If you want it finished — all your pages, photos, and your own domain (yourbusiness.com) — it's $350 flat, half now, half when you approve. Either way, hope you like it!

## Rules

- Mockup = ONE homepage. Full site happens after the deposit.
- Never spend more than 30 min on a mockup. It's a sales tool, not the product.
- If they ghost after the mockup: one follow-up at day 3, then move on. The mockup stays live — sometimes they come back weeks later.
- After they pay in full and their real site is on their own domain, delete the mockup folder.
