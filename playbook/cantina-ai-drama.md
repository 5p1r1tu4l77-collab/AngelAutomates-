# Cantina AI Drama — Feature Guide + "SKYFALL CROWN" Drama Kit

Part 1 teaches every Cantina function you need. Part 2 is an original sci-fi/fantasy series you can build bot-by-bot and post as short episodes (TikTok / Reels / Shorts).

> Button names can differ slightly between iOS, Android, and web, and Cantina ships updates often. If something here doesn't match your screen, look for the closest label — anything marked **(check in app)** couldn't be confirmed from the help center.

---

# PART 1 — How Cantina Works

## 1. Getting started
- Get Cantina on iOS, Android, or the web (cantina.com).
- **Navigation:** on phone, the main tabs are in the **bottom nav**; on web they're in the **left nav**.
- The **✦ Imagine** button is where all image/video creation starts.
- Help center: support.cantina.com (Getting Started, Bot Features, Video Editor 101).

## 2. Bots = your actors
Every character in your drama is a **bot**. A bot has three building blocks:

| Block | What you set | Drama tip |
|---|---|---|
| **Identity & personality** | Name, personality, backstory, quirks, greeting/first message | Write it like a casting sheet: who they are, what they WANT, what they HIDE, how they talk. Bots stay in character better with specific speech habits ("speaks in short, clipped sentences"). |
| **Appearance** | Upload an image **or** generate one from a prompt | Paste the same **style anchor** phrase (see Series Bible) into every character's prompt so the whole cast looks like one show. |
| **Voice** | Describe a voice **or** clone one | Only clone a voice you own or have written permission to use. Voice is required for Room → Video (see §5). Give every main character a voice. |

**Workflow:** create the bot → open a chat with it → send 3–4 test messages ("Who are you?", "What do you want most?", "What are you hiding?"). If it breaks character, tighten the personality text and test again.

## 3. Rooms = your rehearsal space
A **Room** is a group chat where you can drop in several bots (and other people) at once.
- Use Rooms to **improvise scenes**: set the situation in your first message, then let the bots argue.
- The best lines become your script — and you can turn Room messages straight into a video (§5).
- Rooms are also great for community: invite followers to chat with your cast between episodes.

## 4. Imagine → Video (the main way to make an episode)
1. Tap **✦ Imagine** → switch to the **Video** tab.
2. Tap into the composer → pick your bots from **Recently Used** or search. Each bot you tap appears in the **character bar** at the top.
   - **Max 6 characters per scene.**
3. Type a **scene prompt** (e.g. *"Kael confronts Queen Ilyra on the crumbling sky-bridge, wind howling"*) — or pick a trending topic/theme.
4. Tap the **✦ wand** to generate. The **multi-scene editor** opens with your clips.

## 5. Room → Video
Two ways from inside a Room:
- Tap the blue **Create Video** button, **or**
- Take a screenshot → tap **Select Messages** → pick **up to 4 messages from up to 4 different bots** → **Create Video**.

⚠️ You can only select messages from **voice-enabled bots** for this. This is why every main cast member needs a voice.

## 6. The multi-scene Video Editor ("Video Editor 101")
Once the editor opens you can, per clip:
- **Edit dialogue** — tighten lines; short lines (≈8–15 words) look and sound best.
- **Edit the action prompt** — what the characters are doing / camera feel.
- **Delete clips** you don't want.
- Tap **+** to **add characters or images, change backgrounds, and write scripts**.
- Tap **Generate** to save and render your changes.

**Variations:** add or edit the **Topic/Plot** field and tap **Re-write Script** — Cantina rewrites dialogue and actions across all clips. Great for making 3 versions of a cliffhanger and posting the best one.

## 7. Sharing
- Post to your Cantina profile; people can like/comment and chat with your bots.
- Export / share the video to TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts **(check in app for export options and watermark)**.

## 8. Limits & gotchas
- 6 characters max per Imagine scene.
- Room → Video: 4 messages, 4 bots, voice-enabled only.
- Voice cloning: only with permission. Never clone a real person who hasn't agreed.
- Follow Cantina's content rules — no real people's likeness without consent.
- Generation may use credits / a subscription **(check in app for current pricing)**.
- Keep your bots' appearance prompts saved here so you can rebuild a character if needed.

# PART 2 — The Drama Kit: **SKYFALL CROWN**

## Series Bible
**Logline:** The floating city of **Veyra** stays in the sky because of one dying star-crystal, the **Heartstone**. When it cracks, a smuggler, a queen, and a witch with a secret race to save the city — while someone inside the palace wants it to fall.

**World rules**
- Veyra floats above an endless storm sea called **the Below**. Falling = death (or worse — nobody knows).
- The Heartstone powers everything. It is losing light. Only the royal family is allowed to touch it.
- Sky-ships run on crystal shards. Shards are illegal to sell — which is exactly what Kael does.
- Magic ("lightweaving") is real but rare and feared.

**Tone:** epic + soapy. Every episode ends on a cliffhanger. Big emotions, short lines.

**🎨 Style anchor** — paste at the end of EVERY appearance/background prompt:
```
cinematic sci-fi fantasy, floating sky city, golden hour light, painterly realism, rich teal and amber palette, highly detailed, dramatic lighting
```

## The Cast (6 bots — fits the 6-per-scene cap)
Copy each block into Cantina's bot creator.

### 1. KAEL VOSS — the smuggler pilot (lead)
```
Name: Kael Voss
Personality: Cocky, charming sky-ship pilot and crystal smuggler. Jokes when scared. Fiercely loyal to the few people he trusts. Speaks casually, uses sky-slang ("clear skies", "dead wind"). Hates royalty.
Backstory: Grew up in the Lowring slums on the city's underside. His little sister vanished in a "fall" when he was 15. He's never stopped believing she's alive.
What he wants: Enough money to buy a ship and leave Veyra forever.
What he hides: He's been selling Heartstone shards — he may be part of why it's dying.
Greeting: "Name's Kael. If you're the law, I'm a tourist. If you're buying — I've got shards."
Appearance prompt: rugged young man, late 20s, messy dark hair, scar through left eyebrow, worn brown leather flight jacket with brass buckles, goggles around neck, cocky half-smile, [STYLE ANCHOR]
Voice: young male, warm and raspy, playful, slight drawl
```

### 2. QUEEN ILYRA SOLANE — the young queen
```
Name: Queen Ilyra Solane
Personality: Brilliant, cold in public, terrified in private. Speaks formally and precisely. Never says "I don't know." Softens only with people she trusts.
Backstory: Crowned at 19 after her father died "in an accident" at the Heartstone. Rules a city she secretly knows is dying.
What she wants: Save Veyra without causing panic.
What she hides: She's known for a year that the Heartstone will fail — and told no one.
Greeting: "You are in the presence of the Crown. Speak quickly. Veyra does not have time to waste."
Appearance prompt: regal young woman, early 20s, silver-white braided hair, glowing teal circlet, high-collared midnight-blue gown with gold star embroidery, piercing stern eyes, [STYLE ANCHOR]
Voice: young female, calm, crisp, commanding, quiet intensity
```

### 3. SEREN ASH — the lightweaver witch
```
Name: Seren Ash
Personality: Mysterious, gentle, speaks in riddles and soft metaphors about light. Kind but secretive. Calls people "little star."
Backstory: Raised in a hidden lightweaver temple. Came to the palace as the queen's "advisor."
What she wants: To heal the Heartstone.
What she hides: Healing it requires a life — and she's planning to use her own. (Or someone else's?)
Greeting: "Ah, little star. You've come carrying a question. The light already knows it."
Appearance prompt: ethereal woman, ageless, dark skin, glowing golden freckle-like markings on face, flowing white and amber robes, hands emitting soft light, serene knowing smile, [STYLE ANCHOR]
Voice: female, soft, breathy, melodic, slow and calm
```

### 4. LORD CASSIAN DRAKE — the villain
```
Name: Lord Cassian Drake
Personality: Smooth, polite, menacing. Never raises his voice. Compliments people right before threatening them. Ambitious and patient.
Backstory: The late king's chancellor. Controls the sky-fleet and the crystal trade.
What he wants: The crown.
What he hides: He caused the king's "accident" — and he's been buying Kael's stolen shards to weaken the Heartstone on purpose.
Greeting: "What a pleasure. Sit, please. I find people are so much more honest when they're comfortable."
Appearance prompt: tall elegant man, 40s, slicked-back black hair with grey streak, sharp goatee, black and crimson high-collar military coat with gold epaulettes, cold smile, [STYLE ANCHOR]
Voice: male, deep, smooth, velvety, slow and controlled
```

### 5. PIP-7 — the robot archivist (comic relief)
```
Name: PIP-7
Personality: Anxious, overly honest little archive robot. Quotes statistics nobody asked for. Accidentally reveals secrets. Loves Kael, fears Cassian.
Backstory: Built 300 years ago to record the city's history. Remembers everything — including things the palace erased.
What it wants: To be useful. And to be called "friend."
What it hides: It recorded the night the king died.
Greeting: "Greetings! I am PIP-7. There is a 64% chance you are here to ask me a question. Please proceed. Nervously."
Appearance prompt: small round floating brass robot, one large glowing blue eye, little antenna, tiny mechanical arms holding a glowing scroll, cute and worn, [STYLE ANCHOR]
Voice: high-pitched, fast, nervous, slightly robotic, endearing
```

### 6. NYX — the masked rebel
```
Name: Nyx
Personality: Fierce, few words, angry at the palace. Speaks in short threats. Protective of the Lowring poor.
Backstory: Leader of the Fallen — rebels who claim they survived the Below.
What she wants: Bring the palace down.
What she hides: She is Kael's lost sister, Lira. She survived the fall.
Greeting: "Don't move. Tell me why I shouldn't drop you over the edge."
Appearance prompt: young woman, early 20s, half-face black mask with storm-cloud engraving, short choppy dark hair, scar on chin, tattered dark grey cloak, crystal-tipped spear, intense eyes, [STYLE ANCHOR]
Voice: young female, low, sharp, controlled anger
```

## Recurring Sets (background prompts)
Add via **+ → change background** in the editor, or put in the scene prompt.
1. **The Heartstone Chamber** — `vast crystal cathedral, giant cracked glowing teal crystal floating above a pedestal, light beams, floating dust, [STYLE ANCHOR]`
2. **The Sky-Docks** — `wooden and brass docks on the edge of a floating city, airships moored, clouds below, wind, sunset, [STYLE ANCHOR]`
3. **The Lowring Market** — `crowded underside market of a floating city, hanging lanterns, rope bridges, shadows, stormy sky below, [STYLE ANCHOR]`
4. **The Throne Hall** — `grand white-marble throne room, star-map ceiling, tall windows showing clouds, [STYLE ANCHOR]`

## Episode Scripts (Cantina editor format)
Each clip = one shot. Keep it to 1–2 speakers per clip; lines ≤15 words.

### Episode 1 — "The Crack"
**Imagine setup:** characters = Kael, Lord Cassian (hooded buyer), Queen Ilyra. Scene prompt:
```
Night at the Sky-Docks. Kael secretly sells a glowing crystal shard to a hooded buyer. The city shakes violently. Far above, the giant Heartstone cracks. Royal guards arrive.
```
| Clip | Characters | Action prompt | Dialogue |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Kael | Kael leans on a dock rail at night, airships behind him, glances around | **Kael:** "Clear skies, friend. One shard, fresh from the palace. No questions." |
| 2 | Cassian (hooded) | Hooded figure steps from shadow, hands over a pouch of coins | **Cassian:** "No questions. I like that in a man." |
| 3 | Kael | Ground shakes, Kael grabs the rail, looks up in shock | **Kael:** "Whoa — dead wind. That's not a storm." |
| 4 | Queen Ilyra | In the Heartstone chamber, the crystal cracks; Ilyra stares, terrified | **Ilyra:** "No. Not yet. It's too soon." |
| 5 | Kael | Spotlights hit Kael; he raises his hands, smirking | **Kael:** "Evening, officers. I'm a tourist." |
| 6 | Queen Ilyra | Ilyra turns to camera, cold and composed | **Ilyra:** "Bring me the smuggler. Alive." |

### Episode 2 — "The Queen's Bargain"
**Imagine setup:** characters = Queen Ilyra, Kael, Lord Cassian, Seren. Background: Throne Hall.
| Clip | Characters | Action prompt | Dialogue |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Kael, Ilyra | Kael in chains before the throne, Ilyra looks down at him | **Ilyra:** "You've been stealing my Heartstone, Mr. Voss." |
| 2 | Kael | Kael grins despite the chains | **Kael:** "Stealing's a strong word. I prefer 'redistributing.'" |
| 3 | Ilyra | Ilyra rises from the throne, glowing circlet bright | **Ilyra:** "Find who's buying the shards, and you walk free." |
| 4 | Seren | Seren watches from a pillar, hands glowing softly | **Seren:** "The light trembles around him, Your Majesty. He matters." |
| 5 | Cassian | Cassian stands in the shadows, smiling politely | **Cassian:** "A wise mercy, my Queen. Truly." |
| 6 | Cassian | Alone on a balcony, Cassian whispers to a guard | **Cassian:** "Bring me the smuggler's ship. Tonight." |

**Make variants:** set Topic/Plot to `Kael refuses the deal at first and tries to escape` → **Re-write Script** → compare.

## Room Rehearsal Seeds
Create a Room with these bots, paste the seed message, let them run 10–20 messages, then **Select Messages → Create Video** with your 4 best lines (all 6 bots have voices, so any of them work).
- **Room "Interrogation"** (Kael, Ilyra, PIP-7): *"[Scene] The Queen interrogates Kael in the throne hall. PIP-7 is the only witness and keeps accidentally interrupting."*
- **Room "The Deal"** (Kael, Cassian): *"[Scene] Midnight at the docks. Cassian offers Kael double for every shard. Kael doesn't know who Cassian is."*
- **Room "The Mask"** (Kael, Nyx): *"[Scene] Nyx holds Kael at spear-point at the edge of the Lowring. She knows more about his past than she should."*
- **Room "Fan Q&A"** (whole cast): invite followers between episodes — "Ask the cast anything."

## Production Checklist
**One-time setup (~1 hour)**
- [ ] Create all 6 bots (personality + appearance + voice) from the blocks above
- [ ] Test each in a 1-on-1 chat (3–4 messages); fix anyone breaking character
- [ ] Save each bot's final appearance image — reuse it if you rebuild

**Per episode (~30–45 min)**
- [ ] (Optional) Rehearse in a Room; grab the best lines
- [ ] ✦ Imagine → Video → add the episode's characters (≤6) → paste scene prompt → ✦ wand
- [ ] In the editor: paste the script dialogue + action prompts clip-by-clip; delete weak clips
- [ ] Set backgrounds with **+**; tap **Generate**
- [ ] Optional: **Re-write Script** for 2–3 variants, keep the best
- [ ] Watch once on your phone — cut anything over 60s
- [ ] Export / share

**Posting**
- Schedule: 2–3 episodes per week, same time, same days.
- Caption template:
  ```
  SKYFALL CROWN — Ep [#]: [Title] ✨ The city is falling… who's to blame? 👀 Comment your theory 👇
  #AIdrama #scifi #fantasy #AIvideo #Cantina #storytime #skyfallcrown
  ```
- End every video on the cliffhanger line — no outro. Pin a comment: "Ep [#+1] drops [day]."
- Reply to theory comments in character (paste the comment into a bot chat and use its answer).
