# AI Fruit Drama on Cantina — High-Quality Production Playbook

The viral "fruit drama" format (think *Fruit Love Island*: anthropomorphic fruits in a luxury villa, cheating, recouplings, cliffhangers) pulls tens of millions of views — one episode reportedly hit ~39M in two weeks. Most of it gets called "AI slop." The edge is simple: **same story formula, visibly better craft and consistency.**

Everything below is built around Cantina's own tools: Bots, **Imagine → Video**, selfie anchors, and the per-clip **Dialogue** + **Action Prompt** fields.

## Cantina basics that matter

- **Always use Imagine videos, never Fast videos.** Fast videos only let you control dialogue. Imagine gives you action, camera, and sound.
- **Up to 6 characters per scene.** Keep each scene to 2–3 fruits so faces and reactions read clearly.
- **Topic/Plot → Re-write Script.** Set the big idea, let Cantina draft the script, then hand-edit every line. Adding a character and hitting Re-write keeps your plot but tailors lines to them.
- **Each clip = Dialogue + Action Prompt.** Dialogue is what they say. Action Prompt is what they do, how the camera moves, and what you hear. Clear the Dialogue for a silent reaction beat.
- **Use the mobile app.** Image upload for animation isn't on web. Generation can be slow and the app can crash — batch your work and leave buffer time.

## Step 1 — Build the cast (once)

Create one Bot per fruit (4–6 total). Use the **same style phrase** in every description so the cast looks like one show:

> STYLE: glossy 3D animated character, Pixar-style, expressive cartoon eyes, soft cinematic lighting, realistic fruit skin texture

Give every fruit **one signature item** they wear in every episode. This is your visual anchor for consistency.

### Cast bible template

| Fruit | Name | Personality | Signature item | Wants | Secret / flaw | Relationships |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Strawberry | Bella | Sweet but jealous | Gold hoop earrings | True love | Still texts her ex | Coupled with Mango |
| Mango | Marco | Smooth talker, player | Unbuttoned white linen shirt | To win the show | Flirting with Peach | Coupled with Bella |
| Peach | Priya | Confident newcomer | Pink heart sunglasses | To steal Marco | Here for followers | Rival to Bella |
| Pineapple | Paco | Loyal best friend, comic relief | Tiny straw hat | Bella to be happy | Secretly loves Bella | Bella's best friend |
| Coconut | The Host | Dramatic villa host | Glittery microphone | Ratings | Knows everyone's secrets | Narrator / twists |

Bot description example (paste into Create Bot → describe):

> Bella, a heart-shaped red strawberry with green leaf hair, big glossy brown eyes, tiny arms and legs, wearing gold hoop earrings and a white bikini top. STYLE: glossy 3D animated character, Pixar-style, expressive cartoon eyes, soft cinematic lighting, realistic fruit skin texture.

Generate, pick the best variation, regenerate from it until it's right, then save. **Never change this description afterward.** Between episodes you change only scene, action, and dialogue.

## Step 2 — Selfie anchors (every scene)

Cantina's own recommended method for multi-scene videos: generate selfies first, then use them as the foundation for each scene. The selfie locks wardrobe, setting, lighting, and camera feel. If scenes feel disconnected, fix the selfie prompts, not the video.

Template — start with "selfie", then the action, then the setting and light:

> selfie, [character] [action/emotion], at [villa location], [time of day + lighting], wearing [signature item + outfit], [same camera phrase every time]

Examples:

> selfie, Bella crying with mascara running, by the villa pool at night, blue pool glow and string lights, wearing gold hoop earrings, cinematic shallow depth of field

> selfie, Priya smirking and lowering her pink heart sunglasses, on a villa sun lounger at golden hour, warm sunset light, cinematic shallow depth of field

**Lock one camera phrase for the whole series** (e.g. "cinematic shallow depth of field"). Mixing handheld, wide static, and fisheye makes one episode look like three different videos.

## Step 3 — Build the Imagine video

1. Imagine tab → **Video**. Add the 2–3 fruits in this scene (max 6).
2. Write the **Topic/Plot** as hook → conflict → cliffhanger, e.g.:
   > Bella catches Marco whispering with new girl Priya by the fire pit. She confronts them. Marco denies it — then Priya's phone lights up with a text from Marco.
3. Hit **Re-write Script**, then edit dialogue: **one short, punchy line per clip.** Cut anything explanatory.
4. Write an Action Prompt for every clip (Step 4).
5. Watch each clip. **Regenerate only the weak clips** — don't redo the whole video.

## Step 4 — Action Prompt formula

Per Cantina's prompting guidance: anchor the setting in one sentence first, one action + one camera move, end on a camera move or transformation so the clip resolves on a strong beat. Add ambient sound here too.

> [Setting sentence]. [Character] [one action]. [Camera move]. [Ambient sound].

Camera vocabulary: **push in, pull back, track forward, orbit, tilt up, focus shift.**

Examples:

> At the villa fire pit at night with flickering firelight. Bella slams her drink down and stands up. Slow push in on her shocked face. Crackling fire and tense dramatic music.

> On the villa pool deck under string lights. Priya slowly lowers her heart sunglasses and smirks. Focus shift from Bella in the foreground to Priya behind her. Distant pool chatter and a low suspense sting.

> At the villa fire pit at night. Priya's phone lights up on the table. Tilt down to the glowing screen, then hold. Silence, then a single dramatic boom.

Rules:
- One continuous moment per clip. Max three chained actions.
- Reaction shots win — give the listener a silent clip (clear Dialogue) with a push in.
- Prompt one shot at a time and react to what actually rendered before writing the next.

## Episode structure

- **0–2 sec — Hook:** open mid-conflict. A slap, a gasp, a phone screen, a "WHAT did you just say?" Never open on setup.
- **Middle — Escalate:** 2–3 short beats. Accusation → denial → evidence.
- **Final beat — Cliffhanger:** a reveal and a freeze. End before the answer.
- **Length:** short. Split long stories into parts — the cliffhanger is your promotion for the next part.

Story formula: **fruit + emotional problem + villa setting + twist.**

### Season 1 outline (5 episodes)

1. **Arrivals** — Couples pair up. Ends with Coconut announcing: "A new girl is entering the villa…" → Priya's sunglasses reveal.
2. **The Fire Pit** — Priya pulls Marco for a chat. Bella sees. Ends with Priya's phone lighting up with Marco's text.
3. **The Confrontation** — Bella confronts Marco. Paco defends Bella. Ends with Paco almost confessing his feelings — interrupted.
4. **Recoupling** — Coconut forces a recoupling. Ends on Marco: "I choose…" (cut).
5. **The Choice** — Marco picks Priya. Bella walks off — Paco follows. Final shot: Bella's old phone buzzes — her ex is texting. Sets up Season 2.

## Polish & posting (optional, CapCut)

- Burned-in captions (most viewers watch muted).
- "Part X" title on the first frame. Keep the same series title every time.
- End card question for comments: "Who should Bella pick? 👇"
- Freeze-frame + dramatic sting on the cliffhanger.
- Post as a numbered series on a consistent schedule, same cast, same thumbnail style.

## Content line

Skip the trend's worst material — sexual assault storylines and racial stereotypes. It's a takedown and brand risk, and the drama works fine on cheating, jealousy, secrets, and recouplings.

## Quality checklist (before export)

- [ ] Every character matches its Bot description and has its signature item
- [ ] Same camera phrase across every selfie and scene
- [ ] Hook lands in the first 2 seconds
- [ ] Every clip has one action + one camera move, setting stated first
- [ ] At least one silent reaction push-in
- [ ] Dialogue: one short line per clip, no exposition
- [ ] Ends on an unresolved cliffhanger
- [ ] Ambient sound/music fits each clip
- [ ] Weak clips regenerated individually
- [ ] Captions + "Part X" + comment question added
