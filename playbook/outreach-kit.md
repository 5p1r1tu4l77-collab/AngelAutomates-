# Outreach Kit — Landing Your First Website Clients

## The offer (memorize this)

> "I build clean, mobile-friendly websites for local businesses — $350 flat, done in under a week, hosting included free. Half up front, half when you approve it. If you don't love it, you don't pay the second half."

Why it works: small businesses hate agencies ($3k+, slow) and DIY builders (their time). You're fast, cheap, and concrete.

## Building your daily target list (10-15 leads, ~10 min)

1. Open Google Maps. Search a business category + your town (or nearby towns).
   Best categories: restaurants, contractors/roofers/landscapers, salons/barbers, auto shops, cleaners, dentists.
2. Look for businesses that are clearly active (recent reviews) but have:
   - **No website at all** (best lead — Maps shows no site link), or
   - A Facebook page instead of a website, or
   - A website that's broken, ancient, or awful on a phone (open it on your phone).
3. Log each into `lead-tracker.csv`: name, category, phone/email, what's wrong with their web presence.

Priority order: no website > Facebook-only > bad website. "No website + 50 good reviews" is a business making money that's invisible online — easiest yes.

### Template 4 — Follow-up (send 3 days after no reply; ~half of all yeses come from follow-ups)

> Hi [name], just floating this back up — I'd still love to build that free mockup for [Business Name]. One day, zero cost, and you'll at least see what a modern site for your business could look like. Worth a look?

Send ONE follow-up. Two max. Then move on.

## The free-mockup close (your secret weapon)

When anyone shows interest: build the mockup (Claude builds it in an hour), deploy it live, send the real URL.
"Here's your homepage, live: [link]. If you want it finished — all pages, your photos, connected to a proper domain — it's $350, half now, half when you approve."
Seeing *their own business* live converts far better than any pitch.

## Handling common replies

- **"How much monthly?"** → "Zero. Hosting is free. Only cost after the $350 is a domain (~$12/year, paid straight to the registrar, not me)."
- **"I have a nephew who does websites"** → "Great! If it's not done in a month, my offer stands."
- **"Is this a scam?"** → "Fair question. I'm local, here's my portfolio [link], and you pay the second half only after you approve the finished site."
- **"Can you also do [X]?"** (booking, menus, review requests) → "Yes" — then bring it to Claude. Automations are upsells: $100-250 each.

## Rules

- 10-15 messages/day, every day. Consistency beats volume.
- Log every send in `lead-tracker.csv` the moment you send it.
- Reply to interested leads within an hour if you can — speed wins deals.
- Never pay for leads, ads, or tools. This whole pipeline is $0.
