# Pricing Sheet — Angel Automates

Flat prices. No hourly billing, no monthly retainers. Half up front, half on approval. Client only ever pays for their own domain (~$12/yr, direct to registrar).

## Core offers

| Offer | Price | What they get | Your delivery time |
|-------|-------|---------------|--------------------|
| **Starter Site** | $350 | 1-4 page mobile-friendly website: home, services/menu, about, contact. Free hosting. Live in under a week. | 1-2 evenings with Claude |
| **Site Rebuild** | $350 | Their existing content, rebuilt modern + mobile-friendly. | 1-2 evenings |
| **Rush (48hr)** | +$100 | Same, delivered in 2 days. | Same, just sooner |
| **Visibility Report** | $129 | One-page written audit of their whole online presence — scorecard, what each gap costs, prioritized plan. Free with any site; credited toward work if they buy within 30 days. | ~15 min with Claude |

The **Visibility Report** is also your best cold-outreach opener — a free 3-bullet "mini-audit" gets the reply, the full report (or a free mockup) closes. Full playbook in `audit-kit.md`.

## Upsells (offer AFTER they've said yes to a site)

| Upsell | Price | Notes |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Google Business Profile setup/cleanup | $100 | Photos, hours, categories, description. 30-60 min of work. |
| Contact/quote form → their email | $75 | Free tier of a form service. |
| Online menu / price list page | $75 | Extra structured page. |
| Review-request setup | $150 | QR code + short link + SMS/email template that asks happy customers for Google reviews. |
| Monthly care plan | $50/mo | Edits within 48h, content updates, "your guy on call." Recurring revenue — offer to every client at handoff. |

## Money rules

1. **50% deposit before work starts.** No exceptions, even for nice people. (Free mockup = 1 homepage, built before money changes hands — that's the sales cost, and its limit.)
2. Get paid by whatever's instant and free: Zelle, Venmo, CashApp, PayPal.
3. Approval = a text/email saying "looks good." Then final 50%, then you connect their domain.
4. Scope is what's written in the deal message. Anything extra: "Absolutely — that'd be $X on top."

## Path math

- 1 client/week at $350 = ~$1,400/mo on top of platform income
- Each client ≈ 10-20 hrs of survey work. Track B is where the real money is.
- 10 clients on the $50/mo care plan = $500/mo recurring for a few hours of edits.
