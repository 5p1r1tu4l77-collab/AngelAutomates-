## Act 1 — The Will (eps 1–10)

1. **The Reading** — At Harold's funeral reception, Vanessa throws wine on Lena and fires her in front of 50 guests. Okafor arrives to read the will. *Cliff:* "The sole heir to the Whitmore estate is… Lena Reyes."
2. **The Condition** — Chaos. Preston vows to contest. Okafor reveals the condition: Lena must live in the mansion 30 days, and the family may stay too. *Cliff:* Lena drops a photo — her mother as a young woman, in Harold's arms.
3. **First Breakfast** — Lena sits at the head of the table. Vanessa orders the staff to serve her last; the staff (led by Rosa) serve her first. *Cliff:* Preston on the phone in the hall: "Find out who her mother was. Then make her disappear."
4. **The Locked Drawer** — Lena opens Harold's desk with a key he gave her "for later." A letter: "You were never the maid, Lena. You are my granddaughter." *Cliff:* someone is standing in the doorway — Grant.
5. **Not Blood** — Grant confronts her. Lena: "Then we're family." Grant: "No. I'm adopted. They never let me forget it." Romance unlocked. *Cliff:* Vanessa holds up a DNA test kit: "Then let's prove it. Tomorrow. In front of everyone."
6. **The Swab** — The DNA test in the grand hall, live-streamed by Chloe. Lena agrees calmly. *Cliff:* we see Vanessa hand the lab courier an envelope of cash.
7. **Negative** — The result: "No biological relationship." Guests gasp; Chloe's stream explodes. Lena is humiliated. *Cliff:* Rosa whispers, "Mija, I saw the courier. That test was fake."
8. **The Real Test** — Lena and Grant secretly retest using Harold's hairbrush. Chloe catches them alone together at night. *Cliff:* Chloe: "Choose, Grant. Her or me. You have until the gala."
9. **The Gala Dress** — Vanessa "gifts" Lena a dress for the Whitmore gala; it's a maid's uniform in a box. Rosa and the staff sew her a gown overnight from Harold's late wife's fabric. *Cliff:* the real DNA result arrives by email — Lena doesn't open it yet.
10. **The Entrance** — Lena descends the staircase at the gala in the gown; the room goes silent. Vanessa grabs the mic to expose her as a fraud. Lena takes the mic and reads the real result: 99.9%. *Cliff:* the lights cut out — a scream — and when they return, Lena is gone. **(PAYWALL STARTS HERE)**

## Act 2 — The War (eps 11–20)

11. **Taken** — Lena wakes in the staff room, locked in, phone gone. She was drugged, not kidnapped — Vanessa wants her to "miss" the day-30 deadline. *Cliff:* the door handle turns slowly.
12. **Rosa's Key** — Rosa frees her; they find Vanessa's forged documents hidden in the laundry room. *Cliff:* Preston catches them: "Give me those papers."
13. **Preston's Deal** — Preston offers Lena $10 million to walk away and admits the company is bankrupt. *Cliff:* Lena: "Then I'm not inheriting a fortune. I'm inheriting your debt… and I'll take it."
14. **The Makeover** — Lena trades the uniform for the ivory suit (new locked look). First day at Whitmore Holdings; the board laughs her out. *Cliff:* she finds a shell company draining money — registered to Chloe's father.
15. **Chloe's Father** — The merger was a scam to strip Whitmore Holdings. Grant refuses to believe it. *Cliff:* Grant finds his own signature forged on the merger papers.
16. **The Breakup** — Grant ends his engagement to Chloe publicly. Chloe posts a deepfake video of Lena "confessing" to manipulating Harold. *Cliff:* the video hits 10 million views and the police arrive at the door.
17. **Questioned** — Lena is questioned. Okafor proves the video is fake using Harold's real recorded voice memos. *Cliff:* in one memo, Harold says: "If anything happens to me, it was Vanessa."
18. **Poison** — Lena discovers Harold's medication had been swapped for months. *Cliff:* Vanessa, alone, burns a pill bottle in the fireplace — smiling.
19. **The Abuela** — Lena visits her abuela, who reveals Lena's mother Isabel didn't die in an accident. *Cliff:* "Your mother is alive. The Whitmores paid her to disappear."
20. **Isabel** — Lena finds her mother living under another name in a small Mexican town. *Cliff:* Isabel: "You shouldn't have come. If they know you found me, they'll kill us both."

## Act 3 — The Crown (eps 21–30)

21. **The Return** — Lena brings Isabel back to Chicago secretly. Grant protects them. *Cliff:* Chloe photographs Isabel getting out of the car.
22. **Leverage** — Vanessa threatens to destroy Isabel unless Lena signs everything over. Lena pretends to agree. *Cliff:* she signs — with Harold's old fountain pen, whose ink disappears in 24 hours.
23. **The Vanishing Signature** — Vanessa celebrates; Preston is about to sell the company. Signatures disappear at the closing. *Cliff:* the buyer is revealed — Chloe's father.
24. **Preston Breaks** — Preston, cornered by debt, turns on Vanessa and offers to testify. *Cliff:* Preston's car brakes fail on the way to Okafor's office.
25. **Hospital** — Preston survives, barely. Grant confronts his mother; Vanessa slaps him. *Cliff:* "You were never my son. You were an investment."
26. **Day 29** — One day to the deadline. Vanessa has a court order to evict Lena at midnight. *Cliff:* the clock hits 11:58 pm and the front door is battered.
27. **Midnight** — Isabel walks in with Okafor and Harold's *original* will, which grants Lena everything regardless of the 30 days. *Cliff:* Vanessa pulls a letter opener.
28. **The Confession** — Standoff in the study. Rosa secretly records Vanessa confessing to poisoning Harold. *Cliff:* police sirens — but Vanessa has escaped through the balcony.
29. **The Board** — Lena takes over as CEO at the board meeting and exposes Chloe's father live. *Cliff:* Vanessa appears in the boardroom doorway with one last secret: "Ask your mother who your real father is."
30. **The Mansion** — The final twist is revealed (Lena's father was Okafor's late brother, the man Harold once tried to protect). Vanessa is arrested. Grant proposes in the grand hall where it all started. Final line: "I was never invisible. You just never looked." *Season 2 tease:* a letter arrives addressed to "the new Mrs. Whitmore" — from Chloe, in prison: "This isn't over."
