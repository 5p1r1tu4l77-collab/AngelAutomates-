# Series Bible — *The Maid Owns the Mansion*

**Spanish title:** *La Sirvienta Es la Dueña*
**Format:** 30 episodes × 60–90 sec, vertical 9:16
**Genre:** Hidden-identity revenge + slow-burn romance (the #1 and #2 micro-drama genres)
**Logline:** At the funeral of a billionaire, his cruel family fires the housekeeper who cared for him — seconds before the lawyer reads the will and names *her* the sole heir. Now she has 30 days living under their roof to find out why… and survive them.

## Why this concept

- **The hook is instant.** Humiliation → reversal happens inside episode 1. Viewers get the payoff in 60 seconds and want the next one.
- **Underdog + revenge** is the most bingeable emotion loop: every episode she gets disrespected, every episode she wins a little.
- **Bilingual advantage.** Lena is Mexican-American; her grandmother speaks Spanish. The Spanish dub can be released as its own series to a huge, underserved telenovela-loving audience.
- **Few locations.** One mansion (6 rooms) + a cemetery + an office = cheap and consistent for AI generation.

## Characters

### [LENA] — Lena Reyes, 24 · protagonist
- **Who:** Live-in housekeeper at the Whitmore estate for 2 years. Cared for Harold Whitmore through his illness. Quiet, observant, secretly brilliant (dropped out of business school to support her abuela).
- **Secret:** Harold's biological granddaughter. Her late mother, Isabel, was Harold's daughter from a love affair he hid from the family.
- **Arc:** Silent servant → reluctant heir → fearless CEO.
- **Locked look (Act 1, eps 1–10):** *Latina woman, 24, warm brown skin, long dark brown hair in a low neat bun, soft brown eyes, natural makeup, small gold cross necklace, black housekeeper dress with white collar.*
- **Locked look (Act 2–3, eps 11–30):** *same face and necklace, hair down in loose waves, tailored ivory blazer and trousers.*
- **Voice:** Soft, low, controlled. Gets *quieter* when angry. Signature line: **"I was never invisible. You just never looked."**

### [VANESSA] — Vanessa Whitmore, 48 · main villain
- **Who:** Harold's daughter-in-law. Runs the household like a palace. Treats staff like furniture.
- **Wants:** The fortune. Has been forging Harold's signature for years.
- **Locked look:** *White woman, 48, sharp cheekbones, platinum blonde blowout bob, red lipstick, black designer dress with pearl necklace, diamond rings.*
- **Voice:** Cold, posh, every sentence is a blade. Laughs before insults.

### [PRESTON] — Preston Whitmore, 52 · Vanessa's husband, Harold's son
- **Who:** CEO of Whitmore Holdings. Weak, greedy, drowning in secret debt.
- **Locked look:** *White man, 52, slicked-back graying hair, clean-shaven, navy three-piece suit, gold watch, tired eyes.*
- **Voice:** Blustery in public, scared in private.

### [GRANT] — Grant Whitmore, 28 · love interest
- **Who:** Preston and Vanessa's son — **adopted** (secret revealed ep 5, so the romance isn't a cousin problem). The only Whitmore who ever said thank you to Lena.
- **Locked look:** *Man, 28, light olive skin, dark tousled hair, short stubble, green eyes, charcoal sweater over white collared shirt.*
- **Voice:** Warm, a little sarcastic, guilty about his family.

### [CHLOE] — Chloe Sterling, 26 · rival
- **Who:** Grant's fiancée. Socialite and influencer. Engaged to Grant for the merger with her father's company.
- **Locked look:** *White woman, 26, long honey-blonde hair, glossy makeup, pink tweed designer set, phone always in hand.*
- **Voice:** Sugary sweet, then venomous.

### [OKAFOR] — Daniel Okafor, 60 · family lawyer, ally
- **Who:** Harold's lawyer and only real friend. Knows everything, reveals it slowly.
- **Locked look:** *Black man, 60, short gray hair and gray beard, round gold-rim glasses, black suit, leather briefcase.*
- **Voice:** Calm, formal, dry humor.

### [ROSA] — Rosa, 55 · head cook, Lena's ally
- **Who:** Senior staff, Lena's work mom. Comic relief and heart. Speaks Spanglish.
- **Locked look:** *Latina woman, 55, short curly salt-and-pepper hair, round face, white chef jacket, reading glasses on a chain.*

### [HAROLD] — Harold Whitmore, 81 · the late patriarch (flashbacks + letters only)
- **Locked look:** *Elderly white man, 81, white hair, kind blue eyes, cardigan, sitting in a leather armchair by a fireplace.*

## Rules of the show

1. **Conflict in the first line of every episode.** No establishing shots without tension.
2. **Every episode ends on a cliffhanger:** a reveal, a threat, a slap, a door opening, a phone ringing.
3. **Lena wins small every episode, loses big every 5th episode.** That rhythm keeps people bingeing.
4. **Max 120 words of dialogue per episode.** Faces and reactions do the work.
5. **Never use real people, real brands, or real companies.** Everything is fictional.
