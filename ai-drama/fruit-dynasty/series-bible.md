# Series Bible — *Rotten to the Core*

**Spanish title:** *El Patrón del Huerto*
**Format:** 20 episodes × 60–90 sec, vertical 9:16, AI fruit drama
**Cast:** all male
**Genre:** Crime dynasty and family betrayal (*The Godfather* and *Succession*, played by fruit)
**Logline:** The coconut patriarch of the most powerful family in the produce market dies. His three sons, his loyal driver and a rival pineapple boss go to war over the orchard empire. Every episode, someone gets sliced.

## Why this works

- **Fruit dramas are the hottest AI format right now,** and almost all of them are love-island and cheating stories. A **male crime-family drama** is a different lane: same fruit hook, a different story audiences haven't seen yet.
- **Brotherhood, betrayal and revenge** play well with all audiences and skew toward the 18–34 male viewers most fruit dramas don't reach.
- **The fruit is the joke, the drama is real.** Play it 100% serious, like a prestige crime show. The comedy comes from a lemon crying real tears at a funeral.
- **Telenovela DNA.** Mexican and Argentine audiences loved fruit novelas first, so the Spanish version is a full second show.

## Visual rules (this is what makes fruit dramas go viral)

- Every character is **a photorealistic fruit with a human adult man's body**: the fruit is the head and face, with expressive human eyes, eyebrows and mouth, and real human hands.
- They wear **real clothes** in **real, cinematic sets**. Everything is realistic except the fruit.
- **Fruit reacts like fruit:** when hurt, they bruise, leak juice, peel or wilt. Use this in every emotional beat. It's the signature move of the genre.
- **Style line (append to every prompt):** *photorealistic anthropomorphic fruit character, cinematic vertical 9:16, shallow depth of field, warm amber and deep green color grade, film grain, dramatic lighting, 35mm*

## Characters

Paste the **Locked look** verbatim wherever the tag appears. Generate one master reference image per character first.

### [DON COCO] — Don Coco, the patriarch (dies in ep 1, flashbacks after)
- **Who:** Built the Coco family produce empire from one market stall. Fair but ruthless.
- **Locked look:** *photorealistic coconut head with a gray coconut-fiber beard and wise dark eyes, elderly man's body, black three-piece suit, gold pocket watch, wooden cane*
- **Voice:** Deep, slow, raspy. Signature line: **"Fruit that forgets its roots… rots."**

### [MANGO] — Mango Coco, 30s · eldest son, the golden heir
- **Who:** Charming, the public face of the family, everyone's favorite. Secretly made a deal with the rival family.
- **Locked look:** *photorealistic ripe golden-orange mango head with a small green leaf on top, confident smirk, athletic man's body, cream linen suit with open collar, gold chain*
- **Voice:** Smooth and charming, until he's cornered.

### [LIMÓN] — Limón Coco, late 20s · middle son, the bitter one
- **Who:** Always overlooked, always sour. Brilliant with money. The hero of the story, though no one knows it yet.
- **Locked look:** *photorealistic bright yellow lemon head with sharp intense eyes and a permanent frown, lean man's body, black turtleneck, gray wool overcoat, wire glasses*
- **Voice:** Dry, cutting, few words. When he cries, he leaks lemon juice.

### [KIWI] — Kiwi Coco, 19 · youngest son
- **Who:** Sweet, naive, wants to make music, not run a crime family. The heart of the show, and the one everyone tries to use.
- **Locked look:** *photorealistic fuzzy brown kiwi fruit head, sliced open on top to show bright green flesh like hair, big innocent eyes, skinny young man's body, oversized green hoodie, headphones around neck*

### [PLÁTANO] — Plátano, 50s · the family driver and bodyguard
- **Who:** Served Don Coco for 30 years. Loyal, silent, dangerous. Knows where every body is buried.
- **Locked look:** *photorealistic slightly spotted yellow banana head, tall and curved, stoic heavy-lidded eyes, broad muscular man's body, black chauffeur suit, black leather gloves, dark sunglasses*
- **Voice:** Rarely speaks. When he does, everyone listens.

### [DON PIÑA] — Don Piña, 60 · the rival boss
- **Who:** Head of the Piña family, which controls the docks. Wants the Coco orchards. Spiky and theatrical.
- **Locked look:** *photorealistic pineapple head with a tall spiky green crown and a golden scarred face, heavyset man's body, white suit with a red silk shirt, many gold rings, smoking a cigar*
- **Voice:** Loud, laughing, menacing.

### [EL JALAPEÑO] — El Jalapeño · the hitman
- **Who:** Don Piña's enforcer. A rumor more than a man. Appears without warning.
- **Locked look:** *photorealistic glossy dark green jalapeño pepper head with narrow red eyes, lean man's body, long black leather coat, black gloves, standing in shadows*
- **Voice:** Whispers. A sizzling sound plays every time he enters.

### [AGUACATE] — Licenciado Aguacate · the family lawyer
- **Who:** Old, careful, knows the will's secrets. Has a seed of truth inside him, literally (a key plot device).
- **Locked look:** *photorealistic dark green bumpy avocado head, round gold glasses, older man's body, brown tweed suit, bow tie, leather briefcase*

### [UVA BROTHERS] — Las Uvas · the grape twins
- **Who:** Mango's muscle. Identical, always together, always eating.
- **Locked look:** *two identical men, each with a photorealistic cluster of purple grapes as a head, matching black tracksuits, gold chains, standing side by side*

## Rules of the show

1. **Conflict in the first line.** Every episode opens on a threat, a reveal or a death.
2. **Every episode ends on a cliffhanger.**
3. **Max 120 words of dialogue per episode.**
4. **Keep it PG-13 and monetizable:**
   - Violence is *implied*: a knife on a cutting board, a blender switching on, juice splatter off screen. Never show fruit being chopped up on camera.
   - No sexual jokes or innuendo. Banana and eggplant jokes get videos limited and demonetized. The drama is the joke.
   - No real gangs, cartels, countries' crime groups, brands or real people. Everything is fictional.
5. **One "fruit moment" per episode:** bruising, juice tears, peeling, wilting or rotting used as emotion. This is what people screenshot and share.
