# Research Notes — What We Checked Before Launch (Oct 2026)

Fact-checks behind the launch plan. Most sources are creator-industry blogs, not the platforms' own rulebooks, so each finding has a **confidence** rating. Where it says "check in the app", the app is the final word.

## 1. Is the fruit-drama trend still alive?
**Yes.** It started around February 2026. *Fruit Love Island* reportedly reached about 3M followers, and fruit dramas were pulling hundreds of millions of views by April. New fruit-drama videos were still hitting over a million likes in recent weeks. Common formats: soap-opera arcs, villain reveals, family feuds, and reality-show parodies. Critics call it "AI slop", so **quality and real storytelling are how you stand out.** · *Confidence: high*

**What this means for us:** a crowded trend rewards a different angle. Ours is an all-male crime family played completely straight, and most of the competition is dating and cheating stories. Lean into that in the trailer and bios.

## 2. TikTok Creator Rewards (getting paid per view)
- **Requirements:** 18+, about 10k followers, about 100k views in the last 30 days, a personal (not Business) account, videos **over 1 minute**. · *Confidence: high (consistent across sources)*
- **TikTok's official rule:** only "qualified views" pay. That means For You feed views where the viewer **watched at least 5 seconds**, and a video needs **1,000 For You views** before it earns anything. · *Confidence: high (TikTok Support page)*
- **Pay rate:** roughly **$0.40–$1.00 per 1,000 qualified views** (1M views ≈ $400–$1,000). · *Confidence: medium*
- ⚠️ **AI content is a gray area.** One guide says fully AI-generated videos are excluded under the "minimal original input" rule. Another says labeled AI content stays eligible. I found no official TikTok statement either way. · *Confidence: unresolved*

**What this means for us:** don't count on TikTok payouts. Treat TikTok as the place to **grow an audience**, and earn on YouTube, Facebook and the paywall. Show real creative input: our own writing, consistent characters, voices, editing, and behind-the-scenes posts that prove a person makes the show.

## 3. YouTube
- **AI is allowed and can be monetized.** YouTube says channels that use AI tools to tell stories stay eligible. · *Confidence: high*
- What YouTube won't pay for ("inauthentic content"): **generic, repetitive or template-based videos**, and mass-produced AI channels. One source says this is now judged across the **whole channel**, not video by video. · *Confidence: medium*
- One source reports **YouTube Partner Program thresholds doubling from Feb 1, 2027**. · *Confidence: low (single source)*. If it's true, there's a reason to start growing the YouTube channel now.

**What this means for us:** on YouTube, post **each episode once**. No re-cuts of the same episode, no near-duplicate recaps. Every post should be clearly new.

## 4. Facebook / Meta
- AI Reels **can** be monetized when the content is original, meaning "meaningfully created by the person or Page posting it". Duplicates and lightly edited reposts get pushed down. · *Confidence: medium*
- Access may be **invite-only**. Request it under **Professional Dashboard → Monetization** ("let Meta know you're interested"). · *Confidence: medium*
- Reels drove about 60% of the roughly $3B Meta paid creators in 2025. · *Confidence: medium*

**What this means for us:** Facebook is likely the **best payout platform** for a serialized drama. Set up a Facebook Page (not just a profile) on day 1 and request monetization right away.

## 5. The paywall (Ko-fi)
- Ko-fi shop sales: **5% Ko-fi fee** on the free plan, plus card processing (about 2.9% + $0.30). On $4.99 you keep about **$4.30**. · *Confidence: medium*
- Ko-fi supports selling digital products and members-only posts. · *Confidence: high*
- Risk: an unlisted YouTube playlist link can be shared by buyers. That's acceptable at this price. Upgrade to Ko-fi members-only posts or Patreon later if leaks become a problem.

## 6. AI labeling
- TikTok **requires** labels on realistic AI content and may auto-label it anyway. YouTube and Meta have disclosure toggles too. · *Confidence: high*
- **Always label.** Labeled AI content keeps its reach. Unlabeled content risks removal and is the most likely reason an account loses monetization.

## Other risks we planned for

| Risk | How we avoid it |
|---|---|
| Copyrighted music | Only use CapCut's or the platform's licensed library. Never popular songs. |
| Real people / brands | Every character, company and place is fictional (bible rule 4). |
| Violence or adult content limits | PG-13: implied violence only, no innuendo. Written into every script. |
| Looking like spam / slop | One consistent cast, a real 20-episode story, no mass-posting, behind-the-scenes posts. |
| Buying followers or views | Never. It ruins qualified views and can get the account banned. |
| Taxes | Platform and Ko-fi income is taxable. Keep a simple spreadsheet of every payout and every tool you pay for (the CapCut subscription counts as a business expense). |
