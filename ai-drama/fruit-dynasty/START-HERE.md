## Part 5 — Check your numbers every Sunday (10 min)

In TikTok Analytics, open each episode and look at:

| Number | Good | If it's bad |
|---|---|---|
| **Viewers still watching at 3 seconds** | Over 70% | Your first shot or hook text is weak. Re-post that episode with a stronger first shot. |
| **Average watch time** | Over 60% of the video | Find where people leave and cut the slow shot there. |
| **Follows per 1,000 views** | Over 5 | The end card isn't working. Make the cliffhanger question bigger and clearer. |
| **Comments** | Lots of theories | Keep pinning a question on every post. Reply to the best theories, because replies count as engagement. |

Write the numbers down each week, and send them to me. I'll tell you what to change.
