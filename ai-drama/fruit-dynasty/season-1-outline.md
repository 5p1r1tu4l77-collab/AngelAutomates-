# Season 1 Outline — *Rotten to the Core*

All 20 episodes and a trailer are fully scripted in `episodes/`, with Spanish versions of the free run (Eps 1–8 + trailer) in `episodes-es/`. See `START-HERE.md` for the launch plan.

**Paywall plan:** Eps 1–8 free. Ep 8 ends on the season's biggest twist.

## Act 1 — The Funeral (eps 1–6)

1. **Fallen Fruit** — Don Coco collapses at his own birthday dinner after one sip of juice. Dying, he grabs Limón, not Mango. *Cliff:* Don Coco whispers, "It was one of my sons…" and goes still.
2. **The Will** — At the funeral, everyone expects Mango to inherit. Aguacate reads the will: the empire goes to *Limón*. *Cliff:* Mango smiles, hugs Limón and whispers, "Enjoy it, little brother. It won't last."
3. **The Glass** — Plátano secretly saved the glass Don Coco drank from. The juice was poisoned with crushed apple seeds (apple seeds contain cyanide). *Cliff:* There's a fingerprint on the glass, and it's tiny. A *kiwi's* hand.
4. **Kiwi** — Limón confronts Kiwi, who breaks down: Mango asked him to bring their father the drink, but Kiwi didn't know what was in it. *Cliff:* The Uva Brothers drag Kiwi out of the room in the middle of the night.
5. **The Docks** — Limón goes to Don Piña for help finding Kiwi. Piña laughs: "I don't have your brother. Your *other* brother does." *Cliff:* Piña reveals Mango already sold him the orchards, signed before Don Coco even died.
6. **Blood Orange** — Limón finds Kiwi tied up in the [KITCHEN] with the blender humming. Plátano bursts in and saves him. *Cliff:* On the way out, Plátano is hit; juice pours from his side. "Go. Don't look back."

## Act 2 — The War (eps 7–14)

7. **The Driver** — Plátano is hurt but alive, hidden at an old farm. He reveals Don Coco left a second will inside Aguacate's seed. *Cliff:* El Jalapeño is standing outside the farmhouse window.
8. **The Seed** — Aguacate, terrified, refuses to give up the seed. El Jalapeño arrives for him first. *Cliff:* Aguacate swallows the seed, which is the only copy of the true will. **(PAYWALL STARTS HERE)**
9. **Sour** — Limón goes to war, taking over the [MARKET] stall by stall, the way his father did. *Cliff:* Every stall owner he wins over is found wilted the next morning.
10. **The Mango Speech** — Mango throws a public gala, crowning himself the new Don. Limón crashes it. *Cliff:* Mango plays a recording of Limón threatening their father months earlier.
11. **Framed** — The family turns on Limón. Even Kiwi doubts him. *Cliff:* Plátano reveals the recording was edited, because he was in the room that day.
12. **Brothers** — Limón and Kiwi reconcile on the hacienda roof. Kiwi writes a song for their father. *Cliff:* The song is livestreamed, and Don Piña hears a line that reveals where Plátano is hiding.
13. **Spiked** — Piña's men raid the farm. Plátano holds them off alone. *Cliff:* Plátano's sunglasses fall and crack on the floor. Silence.
14. **The Peel** — Plátano's funeral. Limón doesn't cry; his skin starts to peel from rage. *Cliff:* At the grave, Aguacate appears, alive, and coughs up the seed.

## Act 3 — The Harvest (eps 15–20)

15. **The True Will** — The seed reveals Don Coco's real will: he always knew Mango was dealing with Piña. *Cliff:* The will names a fourth son nobody knew existed.
16. **The Fourth Son** — Limón finds the secret son: a scarred coconut working at the docks, raised by Plátano. *Cliff:* His name is Coco Jr., and El Jalapeño is his best friend.
17. **El Jalapeño** — El Jalapeño has to choose between Don Piña and his best friend. *Cliff:* He chooses, and Don Piña's crown falls into the fountain.
18. **Pineapple Upside Down** — The Piña family falls apart. Mango is alone and desperate. *Cliff:* Mango takes Kiwi hostage at the [MARKET].
19. **The Blender Room** — Final showdown between Mango and Limón. Mango confesses everything. *Cliff:* The blender switches on, and the screen goes black.
20. **The Harvest** — Mango is gone, but not dead: exiled to the cold-storage warehouse "until he ripens." Limón, Kiwi and Coco Jr. sit at their father's table. Final line: "Fruit that forgets its roots… rots." *Season 2 tease:* a crate arrives at the hacienda from the cold-storage warehouse. Inside is a single mango leaf and a note: "I'm still fresh."
